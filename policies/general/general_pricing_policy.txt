# General Pricing Policies

## Overview
These policies apply to all products across all categories unless superseded by category-specific policies.

## Pricing Guidelines

1. All prices must be competitive within the market.
2. Prices should maintain a minimum profit margin of 15%.
3. Seasonal discounts should not exceed 30% of the regular price.
4. Price changes must be approved by the pricing department.
5. All prices must be reviewed quarterly.

## Discount Rules

1. Volume discounts may be applied for bulk purchases:
   - 5-10 units: 5% discount
   - 11-20 units: 8% discount
   - 21+ units: 10% discount

2. Loyalty program members receive an additional 5% discount.

3. Clearance items may be discounted up to 50% with approval.

## Pricing Review Process

1. Quarterly pricing reviews must be conducted by the 15th of January, April, July, and October.
2. Price adjustments based on market conditions must be supported by competitive analysis.
3. All price changes exceeding 10% require senior management approval.

## Compliance Requirements

1. All prices must comply with applicable laws and regulations.
2. Minimum advertised price (MAP) agreements must be strictly followed.
3. Price matching is allowed only for identical products from authorized retailers.
