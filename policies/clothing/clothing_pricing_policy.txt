# Clothing Pricing Policies

## Overview
These policies apply specifically to all clothing and apparel products and supersede general pricing policies where conflicts exist.

## Pricing Guidelines

1. Designer clothing must maintain a minimum profit margin of 25%.
2. Seasonal clothing can be discounted up to 40% at end of season.
3. Basic apparel should be priced competitively with market leaders.
4. Children's clothing should be priced 15-20% lower than adult equivalents.
5. Promotional bundles should offer at least 10% savings over individual items.

## Seasonal Pricing Strategy

1. New season arrivals (first 4 weeks):
   - Full price with no discounts
   - Premium positioning in marketing materials

2. Mid-season (weeks 5-12):
   - Limited promotions allowed up to 20% off
   - Bundle offers with accessories encouraged

3. End of season (final 4 weeks):
   - Clearance discounts of 30-40% permitted
   - Additional markdowns for slow-moving inventory

## Brand Tier Pricing

1. Premium/Designer Brands:
   - Minimum 25% profit margin
   - Limited discounting (max 25% even during sales)
   - Exclusive product positioning

2. Mid-tier Brands:
   - 20-25% profit margin
   - Standard promotional calendar
   - Competitive pricing against similar brands

3. Value Brands:
   - 15-20% profit margin
   - Volume-based strategy
   - Regular promotions to drive traffic

## Special Categories

1. Footwear:
   - Seasonal styles follow clothing discount schedule
   - Classic styles maintain more stable pricing
   - Performance footwear follows electronics pricing model

2. Accessories:
   - Higher margins (30-35%) than main apparel
   - Bundle pricing with outfits encouraged
   - Limited deep discounting

## Inventory Management Pricing

1. Excess inventory (120+ days in stock):
   - Progressive markdowns (20%, 30%, 40%)
   - Consider outlet channel if no movement after 40% discount
   - Seasonal storage for potential future seasons
