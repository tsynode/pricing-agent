# Electronics Pricing Policies

## Overview
These policies apply specifically to all electronic products and supersede general pricing policies where conflicts exist.

## Pricing Guidelines

1. Electronics must maintain a minimum profit margin of 20%.
2. Premium electronics should be priced at least 10% higher than standard models.
3. Extended warranties must be offered for all electronics over $100.
4. Price matching is allowed for identical products from major competitors.
5. Refurbished electronics must be priced at least 15% below new items.

## Product Lifecycle Pricing

1. New release products (0-3 months):
   - Premium pricing strategy with minimal discounts
   - No price matching during initial release period

2. Standard lifecycle (3-12 months):
   - Gradual price reductions allowed up to 15%
   - Regular promotional cycles permitted

3. Late lifecycle (12+ months):
   - More aggressive discounting allowed up to 30%
   - Bundle promotions encouraged

## Accessory Pricing

1. Accessories should be priced to maintain a 25-35% profit margin.
2. Bundle pricing should offer 10-15% savings compared to individual purchases.
3. Essential accessories (chargers, cables) should maintain competitive pricing.

## Warranty and Service Pricing

1. Extended warranties should be priced at 15-20% of the product price.
2. Premium service plans should include value-added features to justify higher prices.
3. Service plan renewals should offer a 10% loyalty discount.

## Seasonal Promotions

1. Major sales events (Black Friday, Cyber Monday) may allow discounts up to 35%.
2. Back-to-school promotions should focus on bundles rather than deep discounts.
3. Holiday season promotions must be planned and approved 60 days in advance.
