# Minimum Margin Requirements

## Policy ID: GEN-001
## Last Updated: 2025-04-15
## Category: General

## Overview
This policy establishes minimum margin requirements for all products sold through our platform. Maintaining healthy margins is essential for business sustainability and profitability.

## Requirements

1. **Standard Margin Requirements**:
   - All products must maintain a minimum margin of 30% based on the formula: (Price - Cost) / Price
   - Premium and luxury products should target a minimum margin of 40%
   - Essential/staple products may have a reduced minimum margin of 25%

2. **Exceptions**:
   - Clearance items: Minimum margin of 15% is acceptable
   - Loss leaders: Pre-approved products may go as low as 10% margin for strategic purposes
   - Promotional events: Temporary margin reductions allowed during company-wide sales events

3. **Margin Calculation**:
   - Cost basis includes product cost, shipping, and applicable tariffs
   - Margin calculation excludes marketing and overhead costs

## Compliance Requirements

- All pricing decisions must adhere to these margin requirements
- Any exceptions require approval from category management
- Regular audits will be conducted to ensure compliance
- Non-compliant prices must be adjusted within 24 hours of detection
