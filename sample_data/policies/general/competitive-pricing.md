# Competitive Pricing Strategy

## Policy ID: GEN-002
## Last Updated: 2025-04-20
## Category: General

## Overview
This policy establishes guidelines for maintaining competitive pricing across all product categories while preserving profitability. Our pricing strategy aims to balance market competitiveness with business sustainability.

## Requirements

1. **Competitive Positioning**:
   - High-demand products: Price within 3-5% of major competitors
   - Standard products: Price within 5-8% of market average
   - Exclusive/unique products: Premium pricing allowed up to 15% above comparable alternatives

2. **Price Monitoring**:
   - Key competitors must be monitored daily for price changes
   - Market price benchmarks should be updated weekly
   - Price adjustments should be made within 24 hours of significant competitor changes

3. **Price Matching**:
   - Match verified competitor prices for identical products when:
     - The competitor is a major market player
     - The price difference exceeds 8%
     - The resulting margin remains above minimum requirements

4. **Seasonal Adjustments**:
   - Seasonal products may require more aggressive pricing during peak seasons
   - Off-season inventory should follow clearance pricing guidelines
   - Holiday pricing strategies should be implemented 2 weeks before major holidays

## Compliance Requirements

- All competitive price adjustments must maintain minimum margin requirements
- Price matching should not be applied to clearance or liquidation prices from competitors
- Documentation of competitive pricing decisions should be maintained
- Regular market analysis reports should inform pricing strategy updates
