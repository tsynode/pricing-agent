# Apparel Seasonal Pricing Policy

## Policy ID: APRL-001
## Last Updated: 2025-05-10
## Category: Apparel

## Overview
This policy establishes guidelines for seasonal pricing adjustments in the apparel category, accounting for fashion cycles, inventory management, and seasonal demand patterns.

## Requirements

1. **Seasonal Transition Pricing**:
   - End-of-season markdowns should be applied in three phases:
     - Phase 1: 15-20% discount (8 weeks before season end)
     - Phase 2: 25-30% discount (4 weeks before season end)
     - Phase 3: 40-60% discount (final clearance)
   - New season arrivals should maintain full margin for first 4 weeks

2. **Inventory-Based Pricing**:
   - Slow-moving inventory (bottom 20% of category sales):
     - Apply additional 10% discount after 30 days of poor performance
     - Increase to 20% after 60 days
   - Fast-moving inventory (top 10% of category sales):
     - Maintain full price even during category-wide promotions
     - Consider 5-10% price increase if demand exceeds supply

3. **Promotional Calendar**:
   - Major seasonal sales:
     - Spring clearance: April 15-30
     - Summer clearance: August 1-15
     - Fall clearance: October 15-31
     - Winter clearance: January 15-31
   - Flash sales should be limited to 72 hours with 20-30% discounts

4. **Category-Specific Guidelines**:
   - Basics/Essentials: Limit discounting to 15% maximum
   - Fashion/Trend items: More aggressive end-of-season discounting (up to 70%)
   - Premium/Designer: Limited discounting, maximum 30% except final clearance

## Compliance Requirements

- All seasonal pricing adjustments must be scheduled 2 weeks in advance
- Inventory levels must be considered when applying seasonal discounts
- Margin requirements can be relaxed for final clearance items
- Seasonal pricing should align with visual merchandising calendar
