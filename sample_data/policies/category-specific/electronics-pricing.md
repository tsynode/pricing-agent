# Electronics Pricing Policy

## Policy ID: ELEC-001
## Last Updated: 2025-05-01
## Category: Electronics

## Overview
This policy establishes specific pricing guidelines for electronics products, considering the unique market dynamics, rapid depreciation, and competitive landscape of this category.

## Requirements

1. **Competitive Positioning**:
   - Premium electronics (high-end laptops, TVs, cameras): Price within 3% of major competitors
   - Standard electronics: Price within 5% of market average
   - Accessories and peripherals: Target 40-50% margins

2. **Product Lifecycle Pricing**:
   - New releases: Premium pricing allowed for first 30 days (up to 10% above MSRP)
   - Mid-lifecycle: Match or beat major competitor pricing
   - End-of-lifecycle: Implement gradual price reductions (5% every 30 days)
   - Discontinued models: Apply clearance pricing to liquidate inventory

3. **Bundle and Accessory Pricing**:
   - Bundle discounts should not exceed 15% of individual item total
   - Accessory attachments should maintain minimum 35% margins
   - Extended warranty and service plans should maintain 70% margins

4. **Brand-Specific Guidelines**:
   - Premium brands: Maintain brand value with minimal discounting
   - Value brands: Emphasize price competitiveness
   - Store brands: Price at least 15% below comparable national brands

## Compliance Requirements

- Electronics pricing must be reviewed weekly due to rapid market changes
- Price adjustments for major competitors' promotions should be implemented within 12 hours
- All electronics must maintain a minimum margin of 25% except during clearance
- MAP (Minimum Advertised Price) agreements must be strictly followed for applicable brands
